Planning and implementation phase.

---

# 1. Purpose

DaniniLans is the DaniniHub health/water/lifestyle artifact layer.

Goal:

- build trust-first editorial authority
- explain water and lifestyle topics responsibly
- avoid guru narratives
- avoid medical claims
- use AI-assisted dialogue responsibly
- connect educational content with transparent product recommendations

---

# 2. Core positioning

DaniniLans is not:

- miracle-health marketing
- fake wellness authority
- generic affiliate spam
- AI-generated SEO garbage

DaniniLans is:

- evidence-aware
- educational
- structured
- transparent
- AI-assisted but human-led
- focused on practical understanding and decision support
