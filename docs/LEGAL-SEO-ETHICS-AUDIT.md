# Legal, SEO and Ethics Audit — DaniniNet

## Date

2026-05-26

## Purpose

This audit covers cookies, ethics, SEO metadata, language quality and trust requirements before live deployment.

---

# 1. Cookies

## Current risk

Cookie/legal layer must be verified before live.

If DaniniNet uses only technically necessary cookies, the site can state that no tracking cookies are used unless analytics/ads/embeds are added.

If Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, YouTube embeds, affiliate tracking scripts or other non-essential scripts are added, a proper cookie consent layer is required before those scripts load.

---

# 2. Required cookie pages / components

Recommended public legal pages:

- Cookie Policy
- Privacy Policy
- Impressum / Legal Notice for DACH context
- Affiliate Disclosure
- AI Disclosure

Recommended UI component:

- Cookie notice / consent banner if non-essential tracking scripts are used

---

# 3. Ethics rules

DaniniNet must not use:

- quick-money promises
- fake scarcity
- fake proof
- unsupported income claims
- medical claims
- miracle claims
- hidden affiliate recommendations
- AI presented as authority

DaniniNet must use:

- clear affiliate disclosure
- AI-assisted disclosure
- educational/informational disclaimers
- human decision responsibility
- transparent product status
- registry-controlled affiliate links

---

# 4. AI method rule

Every AI-assisted article, review, page or product flow must follow:

**Pitaj AI — AI pita tebe**

Rules:

- AI may ask up to 3 clarification questions.
- AI helps structure thinking.
- AI does not decide.
- Human verifies and decides.

---

# 5. SEO metadata requirements

Every core page should have:

- unique title
- unique description
- canonical URL
- Open Graph title and description
- OG image
- robots index/follow unless intentionally hidden
- language alternates where appropriate
- internal links
- trust/disclosure where relevant

---

# 6. Language and style rules

Use:

- clear Serbian, German and English
- calm, professional tone
- no hype
- no generic AI clichés
- no aggressive sales wording
- no poor grammar or mixed brand naming

Avoid:

- unresolved old names such as NutriLans on public DaniniNet pages unless historically explained
- non-existing domains
- overclaiming
- vague placeholder wording

---

# 7. DaniniLans separation

DaniniLans should not be described as an active DaniniNet health product until the separate site exists.

Correct wording:

- future DaniniLans health/water layer
- separate project
- domain: daninilans.daninihub.com

---

# 8. Cookie launch decision

Before live:

## If no analytics/tracking scripts are active

Add cookie policy page stating only necessary cookies may be used for site functionality.

## If analytics/tracking is active

Add consent banner before non-essential scripts load.

Do not silently load marketing/analytics scripts before consent.

---

# 9. Immediate next actions

1. Check current legal routes.
2. Add cookie policy if missing.
3. Review app metadata language.
4. Remove outdated health/water wording from main metadata.
5. Check public pages for grammar and brand consistency.
6. Add cookie banner only when non-essential scripts are used.
