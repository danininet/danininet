# DaniniNet YouTube Operating System

## Purpose
YouTube is not treated as an isolated ad-revenue project. Its first job is to create trust and qualified traffic for DaniniNet offers and real-world case studies. Ad revenue is a later layer.

Primary funnel:
`YouTube long-form → Case Study / Opportunity Map → enquiry → €49 analysis / project offer`

Secondary funnel:
`Shorts → long-form or Case Study → same conversion path`

## Channel promise
**Real problems. AI-assisted tests. Measured results.**

Every episode should answer one of three questions:
1. Can a real asset, skill or problem be turned into a useful offer?
2. What is the cheapest sensible way to test it?
3. What did the market actually say?

## Content pillars

### 1. Real Case Studies — 50%
Examples: Čalije Parking, future location/business/service tests.
Format: documentary experiment.

### 2. Opportunity Map — 25%
Anonymous or permission-based cases: skill, asset, experience, available time, constraints → 3–5 realistic directions → test.
Format: problem solving, screen-led, no presenter required.

### 3. AI for practical work — 15%
Not generic AI news. Show a concrete workflow that saves time or helps make a decision.
Format: before/after workflow.

### 4. What failed / what changed — 10%
Show rejected hypotheses, weak demand, bad assumptions and the next decision.
Format: post-mortem / learning episode.

## Publishing cadence
Minimum viable cadence:
- 1 long-form video per week
- 3 Shorts derived from that video
- 1 Community post or poll after publishing if available

Do not increase cadence until production is repeatable.

## Language strategy
Primary language for the first validation cycle: Serbian/Balkan audience.
Reason: strongest authentic narrative, direct connection to Niš case, faster production and lower translation burden.

German and English expansion:
- first use subtitles and localized landing pages
- dub/remake only videos that already prove retention or conversion
- do not split effort across three full channels before one format works

## Video format template
1. 0–15 s: concrete problem + stake
2. 15–45 s: rule/constraint
3. 45–120 s: evidence/context
4. middle: options and test
5. final third: what is measured / what was learned
6. ending: unresolved next step + one CTA

No branded intro before the hook.

## Packaging rules
### Title
- accurate, short, important words first
- one tension/question, not multiple promises
- case number/brand at the end or omitted

### Thumbnail
- one real visual
- 2–4 words maximum when possible
- no fake money, fake occupancy, fake construction, fake testimonials
- use a human face only when it genuinely improves the story; DaniniNet can work fully faceless

## Faceless production stack
Allowed visual sources:
- original phone footage
- screen recordings of DaniniNet / DaniniHub / Case pages
- maps and diagrams created for the episode
- simple kinetic typography
- licensed or original b-roll only when needed
- AI-generated explanatory visuals when clearly illustrative and not pretending to document reality

Narration:
- consistent Serbian narrator voice
- calm documentary delivery
- short sentences
- no exaggerated sales cadence

## Measurement framework
For each long video record:
- publish date
- impressions
- CTR
- views
- first 30-second retention
- average view duration / average percentage viewed
- subscribers gained
- Case Study clicks
- Opportunity Map clicks
- offer clicks
- qualified enquiries
- attributed revenue

For Shorts:
- views
- engaged views / retention where available
- subscribers gained
- clicks/profile actions
- assisted long-form views when visible

Use UTM parameters for every site link.

## Revenue order
### Phase 1 — direct revenue
1. €49 Opportunity Map analysis
2. location/business mini-audits
3. project-specific service offers
4. affiliate links only where the product is actually used/relevant

### Phase 2 — YouTube-native
Fan funding / Shopping when eligible and strategically relevant.

### Phase 3 — ads and Premium
Treat YPP ads as incremental revenue, not the initial business case.

As of September 2026, YouTube lists ad/Premium eligibility for new creators at 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 qualified long-form watch hours in 365 days or 10M qualified Shorts views in 90 days. YouTube has announced that from 1 February 2027 new applicants will need 1,000 subscribers plus either 8,000 qualified watch hours or 20M qualified Shorts views; existing YPP members are not affected by that entry-threshold change. Because of this, the DaniniNet launch should optimize for direct business conversion and audience trust before ad revenue.

- https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009

## 8-week launch sequence
Week 1: Case 01 — asset earning 0 € / hypothesis
Week 2: Parking MVP — minimum cost and break-even logic
Week 3: Website before parking — can digital demand validation work?
Week 4: Opportunity Map case — from existing skills/resources to testable offer
Week 5: What AI actually did in Case 01
Week 6: Market signal update — only real data
Week 7: Second Opportunity Map case or first paid-analysis lesson
Week 8: Case 01 decision checkpoint — continue, change or stop

Each week creates one long video + three Shorts.

## Decision rules
Continue a format when viewers stay, click and/or ask meaningful questions.
Change packaging when impressions exist but CTR is weak.
Change the hook/story when CTR is acceptable but early retention is weak.
Change the offer/landing page when video traffic arrives but qualified enquiries do not.
Stop a case when market evidence does not justify the next investment.

## Non-negotiable credibility rules
- never invent results
- never fake users, reviews or revenue
- clearly separate estimates from measured values
- state when a visual is conceptual rather than documentary
- do not promise income
- show mistakes when they materially affect the decision

That credibility is the DaniniNet differentiator.
