# DaniniLans Project Brief

## Canonical domain

`daninilans.daninihub.com`

Do not use non-existing DaniniNet commercial domains for DaniniLans planning.

---

## Positioning

DaniniLans is the DaniniHub health, water and healthy lifestyle artifact layer.

It is built from existing NutriLans materials and rebranded into a cleaner DaniniHub-aligned system:

- water
- hydration
- healthy lifestyle
- nutrition
- daily routines
- responsible product reviews
- affiliate recommendations with disclosure
- AI-assisted education through the method: Pitaj AI — AI pita tebe

---

## Method

Every article, review, table, diagram, video script and product explanation follows:

1. Problem
2. Context
3. Up to 3 AI clarification questions
4. Human decision point
5. Evidence / practical explanation
6. Artifact output
7. Disclaimer
8. CTA

AI may assist with structure, analysis and questions. AI does not replace human thinking, expert checks or responsibility.

---

## SEO architecture

### Main clusters

1. Water quality and hydration
2. Home water filters and comparison guides
3. Healthy lifestyle routines
4. Nutrition and practical daily habits
5. Ergonomic digital life
6. Product reviews and affiliate recommendations
7. AI-assisted decision method for personal improvement

### Required SEO elements

- title metadata
- description metadata
- canonical URL
- Open Graph image
- article schema where relevant
- product/review schema only when claims and data are verified
- FAQ schema for evergreen informational pages
- internal links to DaniniHub method and product registry
- clear disclosure block

---

## Visual system

DaniniLans pages should include:

- water / droplet / flow icons
- comparison tables
- decision diagrams
- simple process graphics
- disclaimer cards
- callout boxes
- video blocks
- product cards
- review score only when based on explicit criteria

Avoid:

- medical cure visuals
- exaggerated before/after graphics
- guru-style sales banners
- unsupported health claims

## Product / review placement decision

DaniniLans hosts health/water reviews and educational content.

DaniniNet keeps central product registry and commercial overview.

Rule:
- Reviews live on DaniniLans when they are health/water/lifestyle specific.
- Registry and product cards can still be mirrored or summarized on DaniniNet.
