# DaniniNet Monetization Sprint Brief v01

## Purpose

DaniniNet must be built to earn, not only to look good or index well. The goal is to move faster than generic competitors by combining:

- DaniniHub OS method: Pitaj AI — AI pita tebe
- digital products
- mini audits and services
- affiliate partner products
- SEO-ready knowledge architecture
- trust and compliance blocks
- direct outreach and partner distribution

## Strategic position

DaniniNet should not compete as another SEO blog, AI prompt site, affiliate list or guru funnel.

It competes as a structured commercial intelligence portal:

> DaniniNet helps users turn ideas, locations, knowledge, routines and partner products into structured digital artifacts with proof, limits and next actions.

## Revenue ladder

### 1. Immediate entry products

These products should be visible early and linked from the homepage.

#### Digitalna prodaja lokacije
- Launch price: 29 EUR
- Regular price: 49 EUR
- Role: first owned digital product
- Target: owners, agents, small investors, marketers, project promoters
- CTA: buy guide / view product
- Links to: Calije case study, DaniniHub method, legal disclaimer

#### Mini Audit
- Suggested price: 49 EUR
- Role: low-friction service offer
- Target: people with a location, project, product, website or affiliate idea
- CTA: request mini audit
- Output: short structured assessment, not unlimited consulting
- Method: Pitaj AI — AI pita tebe diagnostic questions

### 2. Mid-ticket services

#### Gateway Brief
- Suggested price: 149–299 EUR
- Role: structured artifact for a location/product/project
- Output: public/private content structure, offer logic, trust blocks, CTA map

#### Full Gateway Setup
- Suggested price: 499–999 EUR
- Role: done-for-you / done-with-you implementation
- Output: page structure, copy, trust blocks, forms, product links, email/transaction flow

### 3. Affiliate / partner revenue

Affiliate is a curated layer, not the core identity.

#### UMH Technologie via GrünePerlen
- Category: Health / Water / Routine
- Status: known affiliate partner signal
- Position: responsible partner product
- Claims: no medical promises, no healing claims
- Required links: affiliate disclosure, health disclaimer, water/routine category

#### Amazon evergreen selection
- Category: books, home office, content tools, kitchen routine, practical products
- Position: supporting products, not main brand promise
- Required filter: does it solve a real problem now?

#### Digital tools and SaaS partners
- Category: AI, SEO, content, video, productivity
- Position: tools selected by usefulness, not hype
- Required filter: will the user use it in the next seven days?

### 4. Directory / partner marketplace

#### Free listing
- Role: seed the ecosystem
- Target: local and DACH/Balkan/global partners

#### Premium listing
- Suggested price: 19–49 EUR/month
- Role: recurring revenue
- Requirements: real company, clear category, no false claims

#### Featured partner / sponsor
- Suggested price: 99 EUR/month or campaign-based
- Role: high-intent visibility on category pages
- Requirements: disclosure and relevance

## Homepage monetization requirements

The homepage should not be a generic brand statement. It must contain visible paths to money:

- product CTA
- mini audit CTA
- affiliate/tools CTA
- partner CTA
- Calije case study CTA
- trust/legal CTA

Suggested block order:

1. Hero: positioning + product CTA + method CTA
2. System logic: idea → question → proof → artifact → action
3. Pillars: Income / Intelligence / Health
4. Owned product: Digitalna prodaja lokacije
5. Mini audit / service CTA
6. Partner and affiliate trust layer
7. Old knowledge / recipes / water routine layer
8. Book of feedback
9. Content/SEO categories
10. Trust/legal footer CTA

## Conversion rules

Every page must have one primary next action and one secondary next action.

Examples:

- Home: view products / see method
- Product: buy guide / request mini audit
- Method: start mini audit / view product
- Affiliate category: view partner product / read disclosure
- Health page: explore water routines / read health disclaimer
- Case study: view product / request gateway audit

## No-go rules

- Do not lead with newsletter capture.
- Do not hide value behind forced email gates.
- Do not make medical claims.
- Do not promise profit, investors, sales or health effects.
- Do not create thin SEO pages.
- Do not turn DaniniNet into a random affiliate catalog.
- Do not overexpose DaniniHub backend complexity on the public commercial front.

## Competitive edge

Most competitors separate content, affiliate, product and services.

DaniniNet should combine them in one method-led system:

- question before recommendation
- proof before claim
- artifact before campaign
- disclosure before monetization
- direct transaction before passive audience-building
