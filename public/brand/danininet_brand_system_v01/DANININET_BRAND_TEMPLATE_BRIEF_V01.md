# DaniniNet Brand & Website Template Brief v01

## Final brand decision

DaniniNet is the master umbrella brand.

DaniniHub is the central OS / hub / method / governance / Legal & Trust intelligence layer.

DaniniNet Property Promotion is not the master identity. It is a sub-brand only for:
- Digitalna prodaja lokacije
- DPL Bonus Pack
- property / investor gateway products
- Calije Park Residence proof-artifact references

Calije Park Residence keeps its current identity. Do not redesign Calije in this DaniniNet phase.

Zdrav stil života becomes a separate future health/water channel with its own identity, connected back to DaniniHub.

## New DaniniNet logo direction

The new master logo should be neutral, premium, editorial and scalable.

It should not look like:
- real estate
- construction
- health supplement brand
- crypto / quick money
- generic AI chatbot
- generic affiliate blog

It should communicate:
- structured dialogue
- digital products
- long-form editorial content
- reviews and guides
- trust and method
- DaniniHub-compatible intelligence

## Visual direction for the site

Light-first premium editorial system.

Core feeling:
- warm paper background
- black ink typography
- muted gold accents
- thin dividers
- generous spacing
- serious magazine / digital product studio layout
- calm DACH-ready trust tone
- long-form reading comfort

Avoid:
- Calije dark investor style as the main DaniniNet style
- heavy black blocks everywhere
- repeated generic cards
- circular link loops
- placeholder copy
- hype buttons
- overuse of “artifact” wording on every section

## Website template structure

### Header

Use DaniniNet master logo, not Property Promotion logo.

Header should include:
- DaniniNet logo
- clear navigation
- product CTA
- no oversized logo image
- no cramped nav
- mobile menu later

Main nav:
- Početna
- Metoda
- Marketing
- Affiliate
- AI
- Proizvodi
- Blog
- Legal
- Kontakt

### Home page sections

1. Hero: DaniniNet as umbrella portal
2. Method strip: Pitaj AI — AI pita tebe
3. What DaniniNet covers: digital products, affiliate, AI, reviews, blog, case studies
4. Featured product: Digitalna prodaja lokacije
5. Editorial clusters: marketing, affiliate, AI, product reviews, legal/trust, health/water later
6. Proof system: DaniniHub OS → Calije proof artifact → DPL product
7. Newsletter: one useful question / week
8. Legal & Trust strip
9. Footer

### DPL product page

DPL page can use the Property Promotion sub-brand, but it must not make the whole site look like Calije.

DPL page should be a sales page:
- problem
- who it is for
- what the buyer gets
- why ordinary listing is not enough
- Calije as proof, not the whole page
- Bonus Pack bridge
- FAQ
- disclaimer
- clear purchase/waitlist CTA

### Blog / editorial template

Designed for long reading:
- wide but controlled article width
- table of contents
- author / updated date
- topic tags
- related product only when relevant
- no affiliate spam
- disclaimers for AI / affiliate / health topics

### Product review template

Must include:
- who the product is for
- when it makes sense
- when it does not
- region: DACH / Balkan / Global
- affiliate disclosure
- alternatives
- final human recommendation
